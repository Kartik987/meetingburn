Teams attendance analyzer FAQ

Does MeetingBurn upload the CSV?

No. Processing happens in the browser. Refreshing the page clears the analysis.

Is the cost an accounting figure?

No. It is an operational estimate based on supplied salary, overhead, working-hour and recurrence assumptions.

Why can actual meeting cost differ?

Salary bands, contractor rates, benefits, regional compensation, paid leave, preparation time and meeting outcomes can change the real economic impact.
