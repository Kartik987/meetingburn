A practical Teams meeting governance tool

1. Export the report

After an eligible Teams meeting, organizers or co-organizers can download the attendance report as a CSV. Each recurring meeting occurrence has its own report.

2. Calculate actual person-time

MeetingBurn totals each participant's join-to-leave sessions instead of multiplying invitees by scheduled duration. This better reflects the labor actually consumed.

3. Find recurring meeting debt

Apply a realistic annual salary, employer overhead, and recurrence rate to estimate the yearly burden and model a shorter meeting.

Teams attendance analyzer FAQ

Does MeetingBurn upload my Teams CSV?

No. This version reads and analyzes the selected file locally in your browser. Refreshing the page clears the analysis.

Which columns does the analyzer use?

It looks for common variants of participant name, email, join time, leave time, and duration. If join and leave times are unavailable, it can use a duration column.

Is the result an exact accounting figure?

No. It is an operational estimate based on attendance duration and the salary assumptions you provide. Benefits, contractor rates, regional salary differences, and the meeting's outcome can change the true value.

Is MeetingBurn affiliated with Microsoft?

No. MeetingBurn is an independent product. Microsoft Teams is a trademark of Microsoft.
