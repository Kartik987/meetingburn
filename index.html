Transparent methodology
What the estimate means
Loaded annual salary ÷ working hours ÷ 60 × people × minutes × occurrences
MeetingBurn estimates the labor investment represented by scheduled time. It is not an accounting statement and it does not determine whether a meeting is valuable.
  • Salary and overhead are supplied by the user.
  • Time returned is calculated from the difference between current and proposed meeting designs.
  • Preparation, follow-up and refocus scenarios are optional assumptions.
  • A costly meeting may still be worthwhile when it creates high-value decisions, reduces risk or resolves important problems.


    
FAQ
Questions about meeting cost
How is meeting cost calculated?
The calculator converts annual salary plus optional employer overhead into a per-minute estimate, then applies attendees, duration and recurrence.
Is meeting cost the same as waste?
No. The result measures estimated labor investment. Meeting value depends on decisions, outcomes, risk reduction, customer impact and other context.
Does MeetingBurn store my inputs?
The calculator runs in your browser. Theme preference is stored locally. A shared link includes only the scenario values you intentionally share.
Why model a redesigned meeting?
A redesign comparison helps teams discuss a practical alternative, such as shorter duration, fewer core attendees, segment-only attendance or lower frequency.
