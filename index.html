The Hidden Cost of Meetings, Backed by Research

Meetings are the operating system of modern work, yet most organizations have no visibility into what that system actually costs. The research below draws from published studies by Harvard Business Review, Atlassian, Flowtrace, ActivTrak, and the University of California, Irvine. The Shrinking Focus Session

ActivTrak's 2026 State of the Workplace report, analyzing over 443 million hours of work behavior across 1,111 companies, found that the average focused work session has declined to 13 minutes and 7 seconds — a 9% decrease from 2023.
ActivTrak, "2026 State of the Workplace" — 163,638 employees
⏱

11.3 Hours Per Week in Meetings

The typical employee spends roughly 11.3 hours per week in meetings — about 28% of a standard work week. Atlassian found 80% of workers say they'd be more productive with fewer meetings, and 68% lack uninterrupted focus time.
Fellow Workplace Report, 2024; Atlassian Work Efficiency Survey
📊

Shorter, Smaller Meetings Work Better

Flowtrace's analysis of 1.3 million meetings shows the median duration has dropped to 35 minutes. Only 12% run beyond an hour. 64% include six or fewer participants. Clear agendas correlate with higher productivity.
Flowtrace, "State of Meetings Report 2025" — 1.3M meetings
🌍

Meeting Waste Varies Globally

Asana's 2024 global report found workers in France lose roughly 9.1 hours/week to unproductive meetings, Germany 8.8, Japan 8.3, U.S. 5.2, and U.K. 4.1 hours. These patterns reflect different organizational cultures.
Asana, "Anatomy of Work" Global Report, 2024
Transparent Methodology

How We Calculate Meeting Cost

Every number in this tool comes from your inputs and standard compensation formulas. No hidden variables, no inflated figures. Here is the exact calculation.

Calculation Method

1
Hourly Rate: Annual salary ÷ 2,080 hours/year (52 weeks × 40 hours). Standard used by U.S. BLS and payroll systems. $85,000 ÷ 2,080 = $40.87/hr
2
Loaded Cost: Hourly rate × 1.30 for employer side costs (health insurance, FICA, retirement, overhead). BLS data shows benefits average 29–32% of total compensation. $40.87 × 1.30 = $53.13/hr
3
Per Minute Rate: Loaded hourly rate ÷ 60 × attendees. ($53.13 ÷ 60) × 6 = $5.31/min
4
Session Cost: Per minute rate × duration. $5.31 × 30 = $159.38
5
Focus Recovery Tax: Per second rate × 1,395 seconds (23m 15s) × attendees. Based on UC Irvine research (Mark, Gudith & Klocke).
6
Projections: Session cost × frequency (Daily=260/yr, Weekly=52, Bi weekly=26, Monthly=12, One time=1). Carbon uses ~0.04 kg CO₂ per participant hour.
